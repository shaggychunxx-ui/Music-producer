Clip-edit for music (ported from Mira-Soline VIDEO-EDIT, stripped of adult pipeline).

LOOP
Ping-pong loop with xfade at the reverse turn. Default fade 0.18s.
Do not reverse-concat: that freezes the peak frame.
Loop is video-only (-an). Reverse would rewind audio. Mux the song after.

STITCH
Join A then B. Skip ~0.30s of B (duplicate of A's last frame). Short fade 0.15-0.25s.
Constant fps before xfade or the join flashes black.
Matched action = hard cut (fade 0). Pose jump = short fade. Never fade-to-black.

RIFE
Comfy Desktop :8189, rife49, multiplier 4, 16fps -> 60fps, same duration.
RIFE after the join, not before. Do not ffmpeg minterpolate / MCI.
Skip RIFE if the reverse smears.

GRAIN
Fine grain AFTER RIFE (noise alls=8, optional 0.4px blur). Never grain then interpolate.

QC
Dump frames at 0 / 25 / 50 / 75 / end. Inspect the reverse turn and the stitch seam.

EXTRACT AUDIO
ffmpeg to 48 kHz PCM wav (GROMIT DAW host). Use for sampling, scoring, or S1 Audio Track.

PREPARE S1
Constant-fps yuv420p mp4 + 48 kHz wav sidecar.
Studio One 6 Artist (GROMIT): import the wav as an Audio Track. Video Track is Professional-only.
Studio One Professional: drag mp4 onto Video Track; optional independent wav if you unlink audio.

MUX
Attach a bounced master (wav/aiff) to a visualizer loop. AAC 320k, +faststart, 48 kHz.

OPENSHOT
GROMIT: C:\Program Files\OpenShot Video Editor\openshot-qt.exe (3.5.1, no watermark).
NLE cuts and 9:16 crop. Do not reverse-concat in OpenShot for loops — use clip_edit loop.

REMUX
Copy remux, strip metadata, +faststart. Drops C2PA-style boxes that some platforms flag.
